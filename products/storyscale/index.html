Workflow

From backlog to Jira Story Points, in four steps.

01

Import

Start from a sprint, a JQL scope or a single issue.

02

Compare

Pick the more complex story, or mark two as similar.

03

Calibrate

Use past delivery as anchors, or set thresholds for a new team.

04

Publish

Review the board and write Story Points to Jira when you confirm.

For teams

Built for refinement sessions, not estimation theater.

Product owners, Scrum Masters and engineering teams estimate a bounded slice of work together, with every step visible and nothing hidden from the people who decide.

Before you start

Readiness signals

Thin descriptions, missing acceptance criteria, dependencies and split risk, flagged before they cost you a discussion.

During the session

Live complexity board

Stories move into a visible ranking while estimates stay hidden until the team is ready.

After the session

Session history

Completed and interrupted sessions can be reviewed or resumed in context.

Built to be trusted

Runs on Atlassian Forge

Hosted on Atlassian's own platform. Session data stays in Forge Storage.

Uses your Jira permissions

People only see and estimate the issues they can already access.

Writes only on confirmation

Story Points reach Jira after an explicit review, never automatically.
