How StoryScale works

StoryScale replaces long estimation debates with one simple question: which of these two stories is more complex? Your team answers it a few times, a ranking takes shape, and the ranking becomes Story Points that match your past work.

  1. Import a sprint, a JQL query or a single issue from your Jira project.
  2. Compare two stories at a time: click the more complex one, or mark them as similar.
  3. Review the final board. Completed stories act as anchors, so estimates stay consistent with your history.
  4. Publish the Story Points to Jira when the team agrees.
Before you start: install StoryScale from Atlassian Marketplace and make sure your project has a Story Points field.
