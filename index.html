Our philosophy

Less is more. We prove it in every tool.

Every feature we leave out is one less thing your team has to learn, maintain or argue about. So we keep only what makes the work clearer, and we make that part excellent. You'll feel the difference the first time you use it: fewer steps, shorter meetings, decisions you can stand behind.

Simple from the first click

If a tool needs a training course, we haven't finished designing it.

Sharp where it matters

We focus on the one moment that shapes the outcome, like the estimate that sets your sprint, and make it precise.

Time given back

Every screen we design answers one question: does this save your team time? If not, it doesn't ship.

Our first product

StoryScale

Agile estimation for Jira Cloud

Estimate by comparison, not by debate.

Estimation meetings slow down when everyone argues about numbers. With StoryScale your team answers one simple question at a time: which of these two stories is more complex? As you compare, a clear ranking takes shape, and that ranking becomes consistent Story Points you publish to Jira when you're ready.
