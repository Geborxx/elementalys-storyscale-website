Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

1. Who We Are

Elementalys provides software products and documentation, including StoryScale for Jira. For privacy questions, contact security@elementalys.com or support@elementalys.com.

2. Information We Process

Depending on usage, we may process:

  • Account and contact details provided through support or commercial discussions.
  • Technical usage data required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot our services.
  • Product data required by StoryScale to support estimation workflows in Jira.

3. Why We Process Data

  • To provide and maintain services.
  • To support users and respond to requests.
  • To improve product reliability and security.
  • To meet legal and contractual obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers only when needed to operate our services, or when required by law.

5. Website Analytics and Cookies

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google, to understand how visitors find and use our pages (for example which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, and approximate location at country or city level). Google Analytics sets cookies on your device only after you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is sent.

Google may process this data outside the European Union under the safeguards it provides. You can change your choice at any time: Cookie settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed for operational, security, and legal purposes.

7. Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law. Contact security@elementalys.com to submit a request.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
